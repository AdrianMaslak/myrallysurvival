NOTES
  Intended contacts: flanges (gearbox/angle drive/propshaft/final drive, downpipe/cat/silencers), the centre-bearing bracket,
  hangers, tank straps, pivot brackets and the filler neck's floor grommet and cup bolt to or touch the body; RearHub seats
  the disc, caliper carrier, pads and rim exactly as the suspension set's 4x4 hub carrier does.
  Travel model: links turn about their inner bush, driveshafts about the inner CV, trailing arms about their pivot (bracket
  plates fixed), wheel-side parts move with the wheel; the twist beam turns about its pivot axis per side and both sides.
  Below 10 mm: 4x4 trailing arm to the rim of its body pocket at full droop only, 6.6 mm (L) / 7.9 mm (R); the same as the
  original body (11.3 mm standing). Going lower would crowd the front-drive twist beam just below it (11.8 mm at full bump).
  Engine-set part left as it is: its downpipe flange sits about 7 mm behind the front subframe's rear cross member.
