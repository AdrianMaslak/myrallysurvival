# Art direction

User-selected direction: gritty, simplified realism, inspired by My Summer Car.

- Fictional Central/Eastern European countryside in the 1990s.
- Worn paint, stained upholstery, practical tools and mismatched replacement parts.
- Muted greens, earth browns, faded paint and small high-contrast rally markings.
- Believable proportions with modest geometry and texture detail.
- Readable silhouettes for removable parts and tools; damage must be visible.
- Grounded daylight first. Atmosphere should not obscure road hazards or repair points.
- Original vehicles, logos and locations; references guide mood rather than asset copying.

First vehicle reference: user-selected Skoda Octavia vRS, assumed first-generation liftback. See FIRST-VEHICLE.md. Its 2000 introduction creates an unresolved era mismatch with the original setting.
The game is first person (driver and co-driver in the cabin, on foot for repairs), so the
interior, dashboard and glass need to read well from the seats.
Use placeholders during physics work. Vehicle physics is project-owned (VEHICLE-PHYSICS.md),
so visual meshes only need separate wheels with centred pivots.

Initial performance target is modest hardware: this development PC has 4 GiB VRAM.
Measure costs before adding dense foliage, large textures or expensive lighting.
