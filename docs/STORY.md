# Story so far

GAME-PLAN.md's premise, in its first form: 1996, the factory shut, a written-off liftback bought with a loan
from Varga Holding, the firm that bought the factory; Mr Varga's works team (Varga / Szabo) is the fastest
rival in the regional cup.

- **The loan** (`ARallyTeamState`): 60 000 Sk owed. Every seventh day at 08:00 (day 8, 15, ...) 2 500 Sk
  comes out of the kitty. If the kitty cannot pay, the payment is added to the debt with a tenth on top. The
  debt and the next payment day show at the bottom right. Saved with the team.
- **Letters**: the story's voice, on a sheet of paper in the middle of the screen for 25 s (Enter puts it
  away): the first morning's letter that sets it all up, Varga Holding's telegram after the first stage,
  and a note on each payment made or missed.
- Not yet: what happens when the debt keeps growing (the plan: hardcore but fair), the season's calendar
  and the regional cup's standings, the other characters, the epilogue.
- Test: the shop test moves the clock to the first payment day and checks the payment.
