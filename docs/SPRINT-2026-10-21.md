# Sprint summary: the v2 Octavia comes alive (20-21 October 2026)

Branch `claude/gifted-brahmagupta-8uwvkt`, 45 commits ahead of `master`. Everything below is code, scripts and
docs; the rebuilt Unreal assets come from running `scripts\Rebuild-BodyV2.ps1` on the Windows PC. Nothing was
compiled in Unreal from the cloud session: the pure-rules tests (`Tests/PartConditionRulesTests.cpp`, 264
checks) pass, the rest was tested by playing.

## How to get it running

1. Close the Unreal Editor (Live Coding blocks the build).
2. `git pull origin claude/gifted-brahmagupta-8uwvkt`
3. `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\scripts\Rebuild-BodyV2.ps1` (build, game textures, Blender
   export, the three Unreal imports). `scripts\Build.ps1` alone is enough when only C++ changed.
4. Co-op on one PC: Play options, Number of Players 2, Net Mode Play As Listen Server.

## The car's looks

- **Less plastic, more worn car**: rougher satin paint, duller chrome, matt interior plastics, cloth and carpet
  (`scripts/build_game_textures.py`); smoother paint shading on the v2 body without moving a vertex.
- **Lamps**: every lamp is glass over a chrome reflector (headlamps clear, tail and brake reflectors red,
  indicators amber, coloured lenses see-through); only the right tail lamp reverses, dimmer than before.
- **Grille**: black plastic slats and a chrome-and-green Skoda badge instead of body colour.
- **Mirrors**: both door mirrors and the rear-view mirror reflect (scene captures, drawn while a local player
  is in or near the car; the editor's camera gizmos hidden).
- **Fitment**: wheel spacers (1.5 cm front, 4.5 cm rear): the tyres sit nearly flush with the arches.
- **Mud**: climbs the car on gravel, washed off by rain, saved; `RallyMud <0..1>` and `RallyPaint <r g b>` in
  the console, on foot or seated.

## The cabin

- Working lamps, indicators (self-cancelling), hazards, high beam, wipers (sweep, intermittent, rain on the
  glass they clear), horn, radio, interior lamp (comes on with a front door open).
- Live cluster: speedometer, rev counter, temperature and fuel needles, warning lamps, dial lighting.
- Pedals, gear lever, handbrake and stalks move; the driver's feet work the pedals (left foot rests on the
  floor by the clutch).
- Reach for controls and click them; centre stack reads as a radio and heater, with a 90s crew's things.
- Glove box lid and sun visors removed (they sat wrong and clipped).
- Steering wheel and the driver's hands follow the driver's input even when a front arm has snapped.

## Your own body

- First person shows your chest, legs and boots standing, crawling and driving; seated, the chest folds away
  from the eye so you do not see inside yourself.

## Crash damage

- **Chunky crumples**: dents fold in flat 12 cm facets with straight creases (low-poly look).
- **Side hits** collapse the corner they reach: the wheel leans in, tucks under the body and drags; the car
  pulls hard or will not drive.
- **Windscreen**: cracks show as spider-web lines; a crushed frame (glass pushed 7 cm or roof 18 cm) drops it
  out as a loose pane; an open bonnet blown back at speed cracks or caves it in.
- **Radiator** crumples (up to 18 cm) without vanishing into the engine; its fans move back with it.
- **Engine**: any hit of about 25 km/h of sudden speed change stalls it (turn the key); a very hard crash
  hurts it wherever it lands (80 km/h into a wall takes about a third).
- **Bolts ride with their bent panels**, and every panel bolt was measured onto the v2 body's real surfaces
  (wing bolts no longer float, bumper bolts no longer sit inside the cover).

## Doors, hinges and straps

- Door checks are detents: hard acceleration or wind from about 30 km/h swings an open door shut; braking or
  reversing hard flings it open. Worn hinges sag and hold the door more weakly.
- **Jammed door**: Y climbs across to the other front seat (and out its door), F kicks it from inside (may
  free it, wears the hinges), E pries it from outside (better with the hammer), or climb through the
  windscreen once it has fallen out.
- **Straps by hand**: B hooks a strap on the hanging panel, B again on the body; the prompt says how well it
  will hold (pulling the panel in is best, along it is poor). Drawn as orange webbing; a bad strap wears
  through sooner and lets the panel flap.

## Tools and fluids

- **Hammer** on a bent suspension arm: a blow at a time up to a 60% bodge, a quarter of blows bend it further.
  Hammering body panels is built but switched off (`Rally::Hammer::BodyWork`) until it is good enough.
- **Fuel**: a 20 L fuel can in the boot kit, poured in at the fuel flap on the right rear wing; the service
  yard fills the tank (or the can) at 30 Sk a litre. Older saves get a can on load.

## Pipeline

- `scripts\Rebuild-BodyV2.ps1` runs everything after a pull in one go.
- Moving cabin parts are their own meshes; mirror glass, grille slats and badge are split into their own
  surfaces at export.
